# Welcome to the Line: Precision, Tuning, and Tech

Welcome to the blog! I'm thrilled to finally have a dedicated space to document my journey on and off the shooting line. Whether you are a seasoned competitor, a weekend club shooter, or just someone fascinated by the mechanics of the sport, I'm glad you've found your way here.

For me, archery has always been an endless pursuit of consistency and that elusive perfect tune. Most days, you'll find me focused on Olympic recurve, transitioning between the intense precision of 18-meter indoor rounds and the atmospheric challenges of 70-meter outdoor targets. My current trusted setup is built around a 25-inch Akusta Halo riser paired with 32-pound Bigrock fiber/foam limbs. Once you add in the Sanlida stabilizers and perfectly tuned Akusta plunger, sending those Pandarus Alpha XT 700 spine arrows downrange feels absolutely dialed in. It’s a rig I trust deeply, especially after putting it to the test at the SA National Indoor Championships. 

But this blog is going to be a little different than your standard archery diary. Because I spend my days as a software developer, I inevitably end up looking at target archery through an engineering lens. When I'm not adjusting tiller bolts or analyzing form, I'm writing code. Right now, I'm actively developing a custom archery scoring application that uses OpenCV and 3D triangulation to map physical arrow groupings from photos directly to a virtual target. 

## What You Can Expect Here:
*   **Gear & Tuning Logs:** Deep dives into recurve setup, testing different risers, and the fine art of tuning plungers and stabilizers.
*   **Tournament Recaps:** Reflections and scoring breakdowns from local competitions and national indoor events here in South Africa.
*   **The Tech Crossover:** Regular updates, architecture breakdowns, and progress reports on my computer vision archery scoring app. 
*   **Training & Form:** The day-to-day drills, point-average calculations, and physical conditioning that keep the arrows grouping tight.

Whether we are breaking down the math of a perfect sight mark or dissecting the code behind automated target scoring, the goal is the same: finding dead center. 

Thanks for dropping by, and I look forward to sharing the journey with you. Let me know in the comments what your current setup looks like!
